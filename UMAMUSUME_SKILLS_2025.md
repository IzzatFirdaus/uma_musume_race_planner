## Key Notes

1. **Tier Rankings**: Skills are ranked based on overall usability (SS = top-tier, S = high-tier, A = situational, B = niche). Rankings consider factors like activation consistency, stat gains, and synergy with popular characters (e.g., Daiwa Scarlet, Tokai Teio).
2. **New Skills**:
    - **Angling and Scheming** (Seiun Sky's unique skill) is SS-tier due to its late-race acceleration boost.
    - **Flowery ☆ Maneuver** (Mayano Top Gun's variant skill) and **Eternal Moments** (Air Groove's variant skill) were added in August 2025 and are rated S and A-tier, respectively.
3. **Skill Changes**:
    - **Corner Adept** moved to SS-tier (general usability), while **Corner Connoisseur** dropped to A-tier (more conditional).
    - **I Can See Right Through You** and **Inside Scoop** were downgraded to A-tier due to meta shifts.
4. **Debuff Skills**: Most debuff skills (e.g., Flustered, Frenzied series) are B-tier as they are highly situational and rely on opponent composition.
5. **Recovery Skills**: Skills like **Breath of Fresh Air** (Straightaway Recovery) and **Race Planner** are SS-tier for stamina management in long-distance races.
